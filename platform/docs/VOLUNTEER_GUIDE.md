# Volunteer Guide

Welcome to the Ethnic Health Forum Volunteer Hub.

## Getting started

1. **Apply.** Fill in the form at the Volunteer Hub's "Volunteer with us" page.
2. **Activate.** If your application is approved, you receive an email with an activation link (valid 48 hours). Choose a password of at least 12 characters.
3. **Sign in** with your email address and password. If you forget your password, use **Forgotten your password?**.

## Your dashboard

The dashboard shows:
- your approved hours;
- sessions attended;
- hours waiting for approval;
- upcoming activities;
- announcements.

## My profile

- **You can update:** your phone number, postcode, preferred language and weekly availability.
- **To change your name or email:** please contact EHF.

## Logging your hours (My hours)

| Kind of work | How to log it |
|---|---|
| **An activity you attended** | Choose it and enter your hours. You can log hours for each activity once. |
| **A task or general project work** | Choose the project or task, the date and your hours. |

- **Changing a submission:** you can edit it while it is pending or returned for revision.
- **If it is rejected or voided:** use **Resubmit**.
- Hours for a project you have finished must be logged within 30 days of the end of your service.

## Other pages

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Activities** | The sessions you are rostered on, with date, time (UK time) and venue |
| **My reviews** | Reviews your supervisor has chosen to share with you |
| **Certificates** | Your issued certificates. Open one and use your browser's Print → Save as PDF |
| **Notifications** | Assignments, roster changes, hours decisions and certificates |

## Your data

- You only ever see your own information.
- To ask EHF for a copy of your data or for it to be erased, contact EHF; an administrator will handle your request.
