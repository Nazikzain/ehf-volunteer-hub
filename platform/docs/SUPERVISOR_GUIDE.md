# Project Supervisor Guide

You can see and manage only the projects you currently supervise. If an admin ends your supervision of a project, you lose access to it immediately.

## Dashboard

The dashboard shows:
- your projects;
- upcoming activities;
- hours waiting for your approval;
- past activities where attendance has not been recorded.

## Volunteers on your projects

- Open a volunteer from your project page to see their contact details, skills, availability and service periods.
- **Add a volunteer:** on the project page, choose the volunteer and a start date.
- **End a period:** choose an end date and *completed* or *withdrawn*. You cannot end a period before an activity the volunteer is already rostered on.
- **Notes:** you can add notes for your project. Only admins and your project's supervisors can see them; volunteers never can.

## Activities

- **Schedule:** Activities → Schedule an activity (date and times in UK time, plus venue).
- **Roster:** add volunteers who have an active service period on the project on that date.
- **Attendance:** after the session, set Present, Late, Absent or Excused for each person and press **Save attendance**. You can correct it later; corrections are recorded.
- **Status:** mark the activity completed once it has taken place. An activity with recorded attendance cannot be cancelled.

## Hours approval

- **Approve, Request revision or Reject** (add a comment).
- **Void** approved hours, with a reason, if they turn out to be wrong.
- You cannot approve hours you submitted yourself.

## Tasks, reviews, announcements, reports

| Area | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Tasks** | Create tasks for your projects and assign them to volunteers serving on the project. |
| **Performance reviews** | From a volunteer page, score reliability, teamwork, communication and quality (1–5). Tick "share" only if the volunteer should see it. |
| **Announcements** | Post to your own projects. |
| **Reports** | Covers your projects only. CSV exports are recorded in the audit log. |
